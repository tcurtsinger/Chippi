Frozen screen, v1: two bars beside the selection

The wave 1 board, moved in (Lightshot). Tools run down the selection's side and Copy and Save sit under it, so the pointer stays near what you drew. The bars move to stay on the display, and never overlap the selection.

The frozen screen at 1100 × 640 of a display, in real sizes. The windows, files and numbers are invented, and the frozen screen's own colors (the dimming, white selection, ink colors) are content, not Chippi's theme; the bars follow the app theme.

My pick: it's the flow Travis asked for ("the ease of use of Lightshot"), and both bars stay a short reach from the selection.

Hover: the window under the pointer lights, and a click takes it. The pill names it and its size.
