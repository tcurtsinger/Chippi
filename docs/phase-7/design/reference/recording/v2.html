Recording, v2: a small pill above the region

Game Bar-lean. The setup bar and the pill sit above the selection's top-right corner, so the bottom of the screen (the taskbar, a chat box) stays clear. Sound and Mic are icon toggles, and the pill is a 32-high capsule with icon buttons, so it covers the least.

The display at 1100 × 640, in real sizes, and the island panel at 520 × 360. The windows, times and sizes are invented. The dimming, white selection and red frame are the screen's own colors in both themes; the bars, the pill and the panel follow the app theme.

Pick this one if you record the bottom of the screen often; the cost is icons where v1 has words (each has its name as a tooltip and for screen readers).
