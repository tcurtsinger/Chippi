Clipboard and text: Phase 5 mockup

Clipboard history, Paste as plain text, Clean URL, Scratchpad and Shelf, inside the Surfaces model, with three tiles as in Vorssaint: Clipboard (History and Clean URL), Scratchpad and Shelf. Paste as plain text is a shortcut with no tile. Island and docked pages are always dark; Settings is shown dark and light. Every copy, note, file and picture here is invented. Notes: notes.md.

1 · All features, top edge

Three tiles join the grid after System monitor: Clipboard, Scratchpad, Shelf. Nine tiles make three full rows. Statuses are state: item counts, Off or Paused.

2 · Clipboard, top edge

Recording. The header holds Clean URL (link), Pause and More. Pins first, then recent copies, newest first. The selected copy opens in place with its preview and actions. A password copied from a password manager between "Meeting moved…" and the link left no row and no gap.

3 · Clipboard history states

First use, paused, a picture and a missing file selected, no match, a busy clipboard, and the two limits.

4 · Paste as plain text

No tile and no page, as in Vorssaint: the shortcut set in Settings pastes the copy as plain text into the app in front. When it can't paste, Windows beeps and the closed tab shows the glyph for 6 seconds. It works the same whether or not Clipboard history is installed or recording.

5 · Clean URL, in the Clipboard page

Clean URL shares the Clipboard tile. Its link button swaps the list for the manual Clean: the current copy fills the field when it's one web link, the result shows as you type, Copy puts it on the clipboard. Without Clipboard history the page is Clean URL alone. Automatic cleaning and sites to skip are in Settings.

6 · Scratchpad, right edge

The side edge: one pair of round buttons above, one below, Now Playing on the far side. Editing, with the keyboard in the note.

7 · Scratchpad states

Preview, renaming a note, its menu, a new empty note, 12 notes, a failed save and notes that can't be opened.

8 · Shelf states

Empty, a drag over the empty page, mixed items with one selected, a pinned item, a missing file, a full Shelf and the limits.

9 · Shelf drag, in and out

From File Explorer onto the closed tab, into the page, and back out. Explorer keeps the focus throughout: its title stays active.

10 · Docked by the tray

Island off. The same pages in the docked card, with the footer in place of the round buttons. Crops of the bottom-right corner of a 1440×900 screen.

11 · A 1280×720 screen

1920×1080 at 150%. The tallest page (Clipboard) on the right edge and Scratchpad on the top edge. The dashed box is the whole interactive footprint, buttons included.

12 · Keyboard

Where the focus lands on each page and the keys each page adds. The shell's keys don't change: Esc closes, Tab moves, arrows move in the grid.

13 · Clearing, deleting, removing

Each confirmation is the question, Cancel and the verb. Removing a Shelf item has no confirmation: the file it points to never moves. Uninstalling Clipboard history is in board 14.

14 · Settings › Clipboard & text, dark

Configuration only. Clipboard history recording with two apps to skip; Clean URL automatic with two sites to skip; the plain-paste shortcut in use by another app; and uninstalling Clipboard history from Add features.

15 · Settings › Clipboard & text, light

First use: recording off and no apps to skip; automatic cleaning off (its sites dim but stay editable) while a site is added; the shortcut not set, then set.

Mockup only. Every copy, note, file, picture and app state is invented.
