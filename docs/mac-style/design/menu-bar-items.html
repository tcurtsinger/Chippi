Chippi menu bar items

The right side of the bar becomes Chippi's own buttons. Click one on the desktop to see what it does. Add, remove and reorder them in the Settings section underneath; the bar follows. Windows' flyouts are no longer moved: the two that remain open where Windows opens them.

Menu bar

Top to bottom is left to right on the bar. Drag a row by its grip, or press Up and Down on it. When a bar is too narrow, items leave from the left; the clock stays.

What each one does

ItemClickShows

Only installed features are offered. Battery starts in the bar on a PC that has one, Language with two or more input languages. An item that opens a page opens it in the notch, and a second click closes it.
