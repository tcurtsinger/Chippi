Chippi Mac-style desktop

Hover the notch to open it. Drag it along the top bar: away from the middle it becomes a pill, back near the middle it snaps into the notch. Pull it down to lift it off; a line on the bar shows where it lands. Click dock apps to switch; right-click for their menu. Notepad++ shows an app with real menus; the others show only their name and Window.
