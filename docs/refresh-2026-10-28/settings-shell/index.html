Settings shell: four fixes

The window frame only; page content is next. Sidebar: one list for every group, rows 32 tall and 36 apart, 12 px above group labels, so 12 rows (About included) fit at 1120 × 720 with today's 7 features. With more it scrolls with the mouse wheel, and opening a page scrolls its row into view. Window: no line under the titlebar; pointing at maximize shows Windows' snap layouts; Esc does nothing (it still clears the search box); the window reopens at its last size and place (first run 1120 × 720). Width: page content stops at 1000 px wide, aligned left; narrower than 1008 the sidebar becomes a 48 px icon rail. Scrollbars float over the content: a thin line at rest, wider under the pointer, so cards are 768 wide on every page. Violet accent; invented content.

Settings content fixes

Every page follows one system: 52 px between sections, headers at the card edge (a header's button doesn't add height), rows 48 tall or 60 with a caption whatever the control, dropdowns 180 wide, "+ Add …" on every add button, and one empty state: a caption-color row in the card. Labels stand alone, adding a few words only where they can't. Dependent settings fold into expandable rows with the switch in the header. Add features loses its Overlaps tags; failures show in Caution with a reason. Shortcuts and Permissions appear once a feature needs them. Settings can now do everything the island can (Travis, 2026-10-28): the island's controls sit at the top of their page, and Now Playing, Scratchpad and Shelf get pages. The tray icon opens Settings. Not drawn: Island & pet's preview now shows the 520 × 360 island; raw error text is replaced by plain words; Notifications hides Low battery and GPU temperature where they don't apply; About's VB-CABLE caption matches its Donate link. Frames marked "full length" show the whole page. Invented content.
