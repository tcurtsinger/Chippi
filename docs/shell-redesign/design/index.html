Chippi's shell: Gumdrop as the widget, three looks

Every column has the same shape. Gumdrop sits on the desktop wherever you drag it, with the song beside it while music plays and a badge when the mic is muted. Clicking it opens the 520 × 360 panel out of Gumdrop, toward the middle of the screen; here Gumdrop sits bottom right, so the panel opens up and to the left. What changes is the look. Nothing here is a black pill at the top of the screen, a three-across tile grid, or buttons orbiting the panel.
