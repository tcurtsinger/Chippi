A new blob for Chippi

Five little blob characters to replace Gumdrop. Each is shown idle, asleep (nothing going on), and listening (music playing: headphones on), then at the sizes the widget really uses, in three of the colors you can pick in Settings, and sitting in look A's widget with a song. They're all one flat color with soft shading, so any pet color works.
