The pet on the puck

  • Bigger: Chippi is 40 in the 50 puck, up from 36. The body goes from 26 to 29 wide.
  • Asleep: heavier shut eyes. One z, 10px, in secondary text, replaces two bold z's in #9D9D9D (about 3px tall today). It breathes: 4% taller from its base every 4.2s, with the z drifting up and back. Reduced motion holds it still.
  • Listening: the headphones get a light rim (#E6DCFF) around a darker band and cups. They read on the dark puck and on Now Playing's no-art card, where today's #2A2440 vanishes into #28282C.
  • Wide awake: wide-open eyes and an "o" mouth. Today it's happy arcs 1px thinner than Listening's, so the two read as one mood.
  • The puck's edge: in dark, the rim goes from white 9% to 17%, so the puck holds its edge on a mid-gray wallpaper. Light is unchanged.

The puck becomes the bar

  • Two sizes, never any other: the 50 puck and the 256 × 50 bar. A short title, a long one, a pause, Keep awake and a timer all fill the same bar. A badge sits inside the puck's 50 × 50 square, and hover brightens the rim instead of growing it.
  • One object: while a song plays, the puck stretches into the bar toward the screen's middle. Chippi keeps the puck's place at its end, listening. Nothing opens beside it.
  • What's in it: the cover as a 34 circle, set 8 in so it sits concentric with the bar's round end. Then the title (13 / 600) over the artist (12, secondary) in the 125 between the cover and the equalizer. A title that doesn't fit scrolls, as it does today.
  • Activities use the same bar: the feature's glyph in a tinted circle, and the status over the feature's name ("1 hr left" over "Keep awake"). It turns Caution when the activity needs you.
  • Unfold, 240ms ease-out: the round end slides out from Chippi's end, and the cover and text fade in over the second half. Folding back takes 180ms.
  • Paused: a pause mark in tertiary replaces the equalizer, and Chippi takes off the headphones. 10s later the bar folds back into the puck, so a quick pause doesn't make it flicker.
  • Click anywhere on it to open the page it shows: Now Playing for a song, the feature's page for an activity. A drag folds it into the puck while lifted and unfolds it where it lands, on whichever side faces the middle.
  • Mic muted: the red badge stays on Chippi's end.

The puck, or the bar, becomes the panel

Live: click the widget (Esc or a click outside closes)
  • Where it opens: the panel's corner is the widget's outer corner. A bottom-right puck or bar shares its right and bottom edges with the panel. Kept 8 inside the work area.
  • Frame 0 is the widget, the puck or the bar, at its one size. The widget window hides on that frame, so there's only ever one object.
  • Open, 220ms ease-out: the shape grows out of its own corner. The bar's cover and text fade in the first 25%. Chippi shrinks to 70% and fades in place over the first 45%, and the page fades in over the second half. The shadow deepens from the widget's to the panel's.
  • Close, 160ms: the page fades over the first half, and the shape folds back into the bar (or the puck). Chippi and the bar's content fade back in at the end, and the widget takes over on the last frame.
  • While open there's no widget: Esc or a click outside closes the panel. Right-click and drag come back with the widget.
