Uninstaller, v2: tick apps for a queue

A list rather than a table: each app is two lines (name, then publisher · size · installed), with a tick on the left. Tick several and the bar at the bottom offers "Uninstall 3"; a single app's Uninstall and Open folder sit on its own row when you hover or focus it. Sort is a menu, not column headers. The leftover review comes as a step for each app in the queue, one after another.

The Settings window at its real 1120 width. Coloured tiles stand in for each app's real icon; sizes and dates are sample values, from a list of 312 apps.

Best for clearing out several apps at once; slower to scan by size than v1's columns.
