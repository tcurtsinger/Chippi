Chippi design system

Chippi is a WPF app, so its colors live in XAML theme files; designs/tokens.css copies them under DESIGN.md's names. The island (the widget's panel, the bar, the frozen screen's bars) uses the island brushes; Settings uses the window brushes and the Windows accent, shown here as the Violet choice. Every part below is drawn from the shipped control, in dark and then light.
